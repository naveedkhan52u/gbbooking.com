Accommodation

Explore hotels, resorts, guest houses, student hostels, private rooms and short-term stays for holidays, family visits, work or education.

Confirm room type, occupancy, facilities, check-in/check-out times, cancellation rules, taxes and total price directly with the property.

Transport and vehicle rentals

Explore car rentals, 4x4 jeep rentals, local transport and private transfers for city travel, intercity journeys and mountain routes.

Confirm vehicle model, driver inclusion, fuel, route suitability, mileage limits, security deposit, insurance, permits and total fare. Choose a vehicle suitable for the route and current road conditions.

Tours, trekking and local experiences

Discover sightseeing services, guided tours, trekking and hiking support, trip planning and local destination experiences.

Ask about guide experience, route difficulty, season, weather, equipment, safety arrangements, permits, inclusions and cancellation terms. Mountain conditions can change quickly.

Events and venues

Explore wedding halls, marquees, event venues, meeting spaces and places for personal, community and business occasions.

Confirm guest capacity, date availability, access, catering, decoration, sound arrangements, overtime charges and booking terms.

Home services

Find household support for homes, offices and other properties:

  • Plumbing services
  • Electrical work
  • Home repair and maintenance
  • Cleaning services
  • Painting services
  • Other household services

Agree on inspection fees, labour charges, materials, estimated completion time, warranty (if any) and payment terms before work begins.

Booking, prices and provider responsibility

Displayed prices may be indicative or supplied by providers. Unless a price is expressly identified as final and payable through GBbooking.com, confirm the final amount and payment method with the provider. Availability and response times are not guaranteed.

Providers are responsible for delivering the services they offer and communicating their own terms. For platform help, visit Customer Support or use our Contact Form.
