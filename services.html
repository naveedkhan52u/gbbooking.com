1. Accommodation Booking

Find places to stay for tourism, education, work, family visits and short-term trips throughout Gilgit-Baltistan.

Accommodation types

  • Hotels and resorts
  • Guest houses and lodges
  • Student hostels
  • Private rooms and short stays
  • Family and group accommodation

Check before booking

  • Room type, occupancy and facilities
  • Check-in and check-out times
  • Location, access and parking
  • Taxes, extra guest charges and total cost
  • Cancellation, refund and payment terms

2. Transport & Vehicle Rentals

Explore transport options for local travel, airport transfers, intercity journeys, sightseeing and mountain routes.

Transport options

  • Car rentals with or without a driver, where offered
  • 4x4 jeep rentals for suitable routes
  • Private transfers and local transport
  • Airport and intercity transfers
  • Vehicles for family and group travel

Confirm with the provider

  • Vehicle model, seating and luggage capacity
  • Driver, fuel and mileage inclusions
  • Route suitability and road conditions
  • Permits, insurance and security deposit
  • Pickup details, waiting time and total fare

3. Tours, Trekking & Local Experiences

Plan regional trips and discover local experiences with services offered by guides and tour providers.

Possible experiences

  • Destination sightseeing and guided tours
  • Trekking and hiking support
  • Local guides and trip planning
  • Nature, culture and community experiences
  • Custom itineraries for individuals and groups

Plan responsibly

  • Ask about route difficulty and duration
  • Check seasonal weather and road conditions
  • Confirm guide experience and safety arrangements
  • Clarify equipment, meals, permits and inclusions
  • Understand cancellation and emergency procedures

4. Events & Venue Booking

Find venues and event-related services for celebrations, weddings, meetings, community gatherings and business functions.

Venue types

  • Wedding and event halls
  • Marquees and outdoor venues
  • Meeting and conference spaces
  • Gathering spaces for family and community events

Confirm event details

  • Catering, decoration and sound arrangements

  • Overtime, deposits and cancellation terms

Other Household Help

Before work begins, agree on inspection or visit fees, labour and material costs, expected timeline, warranty (if any), cleanup and payment terms. For safety-critical work, verify the provider’s relevant qualifications.

How to book or enquire

  1. Choose the service category and review available listings.
  2. Check the provider’s description, location, price information and contact details.
  3. Submit a booking request or enquiry where the option is available.
  4. Confirm availability, final price, schedule, cancellation rules and payment method with the provider.

Listing details and availability may change. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, a displayed price may be indicative and a booking is not confirmed until the provider confirms it.
