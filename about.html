About GBbooking.com

Discover and book local services across Gilgit-Baltistan

GBbooking.com is a digital discovery and booking platform designed to help residents, visitors, families and businesses find accommodation, transport, local experiences, event venues and household services across Gilgit-Baltistan.

Our purpose

Finding a suitable hotel, arranging transport, planning a trip or locating a home-service professional can take time when information is spread across calls, social pages and word of mouth. GBbooking.com aims to make discovery simpler by bringing useful local services into one accessible place. We are building around practical regional needs, clear service information and a straightforward customer journey.

Services on the platform

  • Accommodation: hotels, guest houses, student hostels, private rooms and short stays.
  • Transport: car rentals, 4x4 jeep rentals, local transport and travel transfers.
  • Tours and experiences: sightseeing, trekking support, local guides and trip experiences.
  • Events and venues: halls, marquees and spaces for weddings, meetings and gatherings.
  • Home services: plumbing, electrical work, home repair and maintenance, cleaning, painting and other household services.

Availability, provider participation and coverage may vary by location and time.

How GBbooking works

  1. Explore service categories and available listings.
  2. Review information supplied for a service or provider.
  3. Submit a booking or enquiry where the feature is available, or contact the provider using the displayed contact option.
  4. Confirm the final price, schedule, availability, terms and payment arrangements with the provider unless the platform clearly states otherwise.

Our approach

We aim to make local services easier to discover, keep platform information understandable and provide clear ways to contact our team. We also want local businesses and independent providers to have a practical way to present their services to potential customers. A listing is not, by itself, a guarantee of service quality, availability, licensing or a particular outcome. Customers should review provider details and confirm important terms before booking.
