What we can help with

  • Finding a service category or navigating the website.
  • Questions about a listing or contacting a provider.
  • Booking enquiry follow-ups and request clarification.
  • Provider listing and platform-related questions.
  • Reporting incorrect information, suspicious listings or technical problems.
  • Privacy-related questions and requests.

Before contacting support

Provide the page or service name, city or area, date of your enquiry and a short description of what happened. Include a booking or enquiry reference if you have one. Never send passwords, one-time verification codes or full payment card details.

For a service delivery issue, contact the provider directly as well. GBbooking support can help with platform communication and enquiries, but the provider remains responsible for confirming and delivering their service unless a specific platform arrangement says otherwise.
